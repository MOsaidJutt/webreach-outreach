📌 What is WebReach?

WebReach is an automated outreach system that finds local businesses on Google Maps with great ratings but no website, then contacts them via SMS using an AI agent. When a business shows interest, you are alerted to create their website and send them the link.

Step 1 — Find Leads (Scraping)

Go to Scraping Jobs in the left sidebar.

Quick Search (API)

  1. Click the API Search tab
  2. Enter a business type — e.g. restaurants, hair salons, plumbers
  3. Enter a location — e.g. Bristol UK or Miami FL
  4. Click Start Scraping
  5. A progress dialog appears. Results load in 1–3 minutes
  6. Click View Leads when the job shows "completed"
💡 Better Results: Use the Import from Outscraper CSV tab for more accurate "no website" filtering. Export from app.outscraper.com with filters: Has website = No, Rating ≥ 4.0, Has phone = Yes.

Step 2 — Filter Your Leads

Go to Leads in the sidebar. Use the filter bar at the top to narrow down your target list.

FilterRecommended SettingWhy
📱 PhoneHas PhoneYou can only SMS leads with a phone number
🌐 WebsiteNo WebsiteThese are your target — businesses that need a site
⭐ Rating4.0+Higher rated businesses are better prospects

This filtered view shows your ideal outreach list. You can export it at any time using the Export CSV or Export Excel buttons.

Step 3 — Import to GHL & Send SMS

  1. From the Dashboard, click Import All to GHL — this creates contacts in Go High Level
  2. Then click Send Bulk SMS — the AI agent sends the first message to all imported leads
ℹ️ Individual lead: On the Leads page, use the ☁️ icon to import one lead to GHL and the ✉️ icon to send SMS to one lead.

Step 5 — Handle Interested Leads

When a lead says yes, their status changes to Interested and they are tagged in GHL for manual follow-up.

  1. Go to Leads → filter by Status: Interested
  2. Create their website on Lovable (lovable.dev)
  3. Click the ✏️ pencil icon on the lead
  4. Change status to Website Sent
  5. Paste the Lovable website URL
  6. Send the link to the business via GHL manually
  7. When they convert, update status to Converted

Step 6 — Auto Follow-Up

Testing the AI — AI Chat Test Page

Before running a real campaign, test the AI conversation:

  1. Go to AI Chat Test in the sidebar
  2. Enter a business name, Google rating, and review count
  3. Click Start New Conversation
  4. Reply as if you are the business owner
  5. Try: "yes", "no", "stop", "what's your website?"
ℹ️ This is a simulation only — no real SMS is sent during testing.

Exporting Data

Export your leads at any time in CSV or Excel format:

  • Top right of any page → Export CSV / Export Excel (exports all leads)
  • Leads page → Export button → exports only the currently filtered view

For example: filter by Interested then export to get a clean list of all interested leads.

FAQ

Will opted-out leads ever be messaged again?

No. Once a lead's status is Opted Out, the system never messages them again.

Can the same business be scraped twice?

No. The system deduplicates by Google Place ID and phone number. The same business will not be imported twice.

What happens after a lead says yes?

The AI sends a final acknowledgement and stops auto-replying. You'll see the lead under Leads → Interested. All further communication is manual via GHL.

How do I change what the AI says?

Go to Admin → Business Identity to update your business name, website URL, and agent name. These update all future messages instantly.

What if a lead has a website but the filter didn't catch it?

Use the Outscraper CSV import method (Option B in Step 1) for the most accurate "no website" filtering, as it uses Outscraper's native server-side filter.
