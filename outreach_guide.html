🧭 How Outreach Works

Outreach finds local businesses on Google Maps that have great reviews but no website, then contacts them via SMS using an AI agent. When a business shows interest, you're notified to create their website and send them the link.

Left panel — conversation list

  • Search box — filter the list by name, phone, or city as you type
  • Filter pills:
    PillShows

Right panel — message thread

Click any conversation to open it. The header shows the lead's name, phone, status badge, and an AI Status badge:

BadgeMeaning
🤖 AI ActiveThe bot is handling this conversation automatically — it will reply to the lead's next message by itself
🟡 Manual ModeThe AI is paused for this lead — you're in control. The bot will not reply, even if the lead texts back

Action buttons in the thread header

ButtonWhat it does
🟡 Take Over (shown when AI Active)Pauses the AI for this lead and switches to Manual Mode. Use this before you reply personally — e.g. answering a tricky question, negotiating, or any time you don't want the bot to chime in
🤖 Resume AI (shown when in Manual Mode)Hands the conversation back to the bot (asks for confirmation first). The AI will respond to the lead's next message automatically again
👤 LeadOpens this lead's full detail page in a new tab
🗺️ MapsOpens the business's Google Maps listing (if available)
🗑️ DeletePermanently deletes this lead and its entire conversation history (asks for confirmation — cannot be undone)
🖐️ When a conversation is in Manual Mode, a yellow banner appears: "Manual Mode — AI is paused. You're in control. Click Resume AI to hand back to the bot." Incoming texts from the lead are still recorded and visible in the thread — the AI simply won't answer them until you click Resume AI.

Sending a message yourself

At the bottom of the thread is a text box: "Type a manual message to send via GHL SMS..."

  • Type your message and click Send (paper-plane icon), or press Ctrl+Enter
  • The message is sent immediately via GHL/Twilio to the lead's phone
  • Sending a manual message automatically switches this conversation to Manual Mode — even if you didn't click "Take Over" first. This guarantees the AI never talks over you.
  • Your manual messages appear in the thread in green, labelled "You (Manual)" — AI messages appear in blue, labelled "You (AI)" — and the lead's replies appear on the left, labelled "Lead"
💡 Workflow tip: Use the Manual Mode filter pill regularly to make sure you don't forget any conversation you've personally taken over. When you're done, click Resume AI so the bot keeps the conversation moving.

Status & notes bar

At the very bottom of the thread you can change the lead's Status (saves immediately) and add/edit a quick Note (click Save).

🔍 Scraping Jobs

The Scraping Jobs page (/scraping) is how you find new businesses on Google Maps. It has two tabs.

Tab 1 — Import from Outscraper CSV Recommended

This is the default, recommended tab — Outscraper's own "no website" filter is far more accurate than filtering after the fact.

Steps

  1. Go to app.outscraper.com and log in
  2. Click Google Maps search
  3. Enter your search query and location (e.g. "restaurants in Bristol UK")
  4. Under Filters, set:
    • Has website → No
    • Rating → ≥ 4.0
    • Has phone → Yes
  5. Run the search and wait for results
  6. Click Export → CSV
  7. Back in Outreach: choose the CSV file under "Outscraper CSV File"
  8. (Optional) Set a Minimum Rating Filter — No minimum / 3.5★+ / 4.0★+ (default) / 4.5★+
  9. Click Import Leads

Import results summary

After uploading, Outreach shows exactly what happened to every row:

ResultMeaning

🌐 Skipped (has website)Business already has a website — not a target
📋 Skipped (duplicate)Already exists (matched by Google Place ID or phone number)
📵 Skipped (no phone)Can't be contacted via SMS
⭐ Skipped (low rating)Below your chosen minimum rating
ErrorsAny rows that couldn't be processed (first 3 shown)

On success, you're automatically redirected to the Leads page after 2 seconds.

ℹ️ Expected CSV columns: name, rating, reviews, phone, type, full_address, city, state, country_code, place_id, site, url — Outreach auto-detects column names, so most Outscraper exports work without changes.

✅ Common Workflows

1. Daily 5-minute check-in

  1. Open the Dashboard — glance at Messages Sent, Interested Leads, and the SMS Activity chart
  2. Go to Conversations → click the Manual Mode filter — handle anything you've personally taken over
  3. Click the Interested filter — see if any new leads need a website built

2. Finding new leads (Scraping)

  1. Go to Scraping Jobs
  2. Use the Import from Outscraper CSV tab (most accurate) — export from Outscraper with "Has website: No", "Rating ≥ 4.0", "Has phone: Yes", then upload here
  3. Review the import summary, then go to Leads to see your new businesses

3. Sending your first outreach batch

  1. On Leads, filter: Phone = Has Phone, Website = No Website, Rating = 4.0+
  2. Select the leads (or "Select all X leads")
  3. Click Import Selected to GHL
  4. Either click Send SMS to Selected for an immediate batch, or (recommended) turn on Smart Send in Admin and let it pace sends automatically over the following days

4. Handling an interested lead end-to-end

  1. Leads → filter Status = Interested
  2. Build their website on Lovable (lovable.dev)
  3. Click ✏️ on the lead → set Status = Website Sent → paste the Lovable URL → Save
  4. Send the link to the business — either manually via Conversations (this auto-pauses the AI for that lead) or however you prefer
  5. When they sign up, update Status = Converted

5. Personally handling a tricky conversation

  1. Go to Conversations, open the lead's thread
  2. Click Take Over (or just start typing a manual reply — it auto-takes-over)
  3. Reply as needed — use Ctrl+Enter to send quickly
  4. When finished, click Resume AI to hand back to the bot

6. Turning on Smart Send for the first time

  1. Admin → Smart Send Scheduler
  2. Review/adjust Warm-Up settings (Start/Increase/Max) and the Sending Window (timezone, hours, gaps) — click Auto-detect for timezone if unsure
  3. Click Save Settings
  4. Click Start Warm-Up from Today
  5. Toggle Smart Send ON

7. Customising what the AI says

  1. AI Settings → edit the System Prompt and/or Message Templates

❓ FAQ

Will opted-out leads ever be messaged again?

No. Once a lead's status is "Opted Out", the system never messages them again.

Can the same business be scraped twice?

No. Outreach de-duplicates by Google Place ID and phone number — the same business won't be imported twice.

What happens after a lead says yes?

The AI sends a final acknowledgement and stops auto-replying that conversation thread. You'll see them under Leads → Interested. From there it's a manual handoff — build their site and send the link.

What's the difference between "Take Over" and just replying through GHL directly?

Replying through Outreach's Conversations page keeps everything — AI and manual messages — in one searchable history, and automatically marks the lead as Manual Mode so the AI doesn't accidentally reply at the same time as you.

I turned on Smart Send but "Send Bulk SMS" on the Dashboard says it's blocked — why?

This is intentional. While Smart Send is active, it's already sending messages on a schedule in the background — a manual bulk send at the same time would double-message leads. Turn Smart Send off first if you need a one-off manual batch.

How do I change what the AI says?

Go to AI Settings → Message Templates / System Prompt. Changes apply immediately to all future messages.

What if a lead has a website but the filter didn't catch it?

Use the Outscraper CSV import method — it uses Outscraper's own server-side "no website" filter, which is far more accurate than Outreach's after-the-fact filtering.

How much does Outscraper cost?

Approximately $2–5 per 1,000 businesses scraped. New accounts get free credits.
